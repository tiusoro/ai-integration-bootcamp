##### Part 3: The 30-Day Post-Graduation Challenge

##### The Rules

1. **No zero days.** Every day, do at least one thing that moves you toward a client.
2. **Track everything.** Proposals sent, calls had, rejections received, wins celebrated.
3. **Ship in public.** Post your journey on LinkedIn. Let the world watch you succeed.
4. **Help others.** Answer questions in AI/FastAPI communities. Teaching reinforces learning.
5. **Sleep.** 8 hours. No exceptions. Burnout kills momentum.

##### Weekly Milestones
| Week       | Target                             | Reward                      |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Week 1** | 5 proposals sent, 3 LinkedIn posts | Buy yourself a nice meal    |
| **Week 2** | First discovery call scheduled     | Take a half-day off         |
| **Week 3** | First client proposal accepted     | Celebrate with friends      |
| **Week 4** | First invoice sent (\$1,500+)      | Buy something you've wanted |
