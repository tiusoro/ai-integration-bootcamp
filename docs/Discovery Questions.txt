
Step 1: The Discovery Call Script (5 Questions)

Question 1: "What problem are you trying to solve?"
Customer support team spends 4 hours/day answering repetitive FAQ, pulling them from revenue-generating work.  

Question 2: "What does success look like in 30 days?"
Reduce FAQ response time from 2 hours to instant. Freeing up 3 staff hours daily.

Question 3: "What happens if you do nothing?"
Same cycle continues…reduced revenue and increased staff stress. 

Question 4: "Who will use this — customers or staff?"
Customers

Question 5: "Do you have documents — FAQ, policies, product info?"
 FAQ
